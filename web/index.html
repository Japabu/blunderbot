Credit the move.
Not the opponent’s mistake.

Each move is compared with the engine’s evaluation immediately before you played it. An opponent blundering no longer earns you a good-move reaction by itself.

ReactionWhat earns it
BrilliantA verified sound piece or exchange sacrifice
GreatThe only good option; alternatives lose at least 10 points
Normal good / bestQuiet — finding a routine move needs no fanfare
Inaccuracy5–10 percentage points lost
Mistake10–20 points lost
Blunder20+ points lost

Blunderbot uses Chess.com’s published loss bands with Lichess’s public conversion from engine scores to winning chances. It does not reproduce Chess.com’s private rating-adjusted model.

Chess.com also rewards pivotal “Great” moves and sound sacrifices as “Brilliant.” Blunderbot verifies Brilliant candidates with a real piece or exchange sacrifice, depth-12+ searches, an alternative move that is not already easily winning, and sound compensation if the sacrifice is accepted. Extra searches run only for sacrifice candidates. Great moves must be near-best, keep a playable position, and beat the next-best option by at least 10 percentage points at depth 12+. Normal good, excellent and best moves have no positive reaction. The fast-good-move category is removed.
